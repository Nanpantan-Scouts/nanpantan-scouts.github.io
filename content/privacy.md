This page explains what happens to your information when you use this website. It's run by 1st Nanpantan Scout Group, Nanpantan Road, Loughborough LE11 3YE.

## What this website collects

This website doesn't use analytics or tracking, doesn't set any cookies of its own, and doesn't ask you to log in or fill in forms on the site itself.

## Other services we use

Some parts of the site come from other companies. When you view those parts, your browser connects to them, and they may collect information such as your IP address or set their own cookies, under their own privacy policies.

- **Facebook:** the feed of our Facebook posts on the homepage. Messages you send us through Facebook or Messenger are handled by Facebook.
- **Jotform:** the venue hire booking and enquiry forms. We receive what you enter so we can reply and manage your booking.
- **Microsoft Outlook:** the venue availability calendar.
- **Flickr:** photo albums embedded in some news posts.
- **Google Fonts, Font Awesome, Bootstrap and jQuery:** fonts, icons and page code loaded from their servers.
- **Google Search:** the search box on news pages sends your search to Google.
- **GitHub Pages:** hosts this website, and may keep technical logs such as IP addresses for security.

## If you contact us

If you message us on Facebook, email us or make a booking enquiry, we use your details only to reply to you and to deal with your enquiry or booking. We don't sell or share them for marketing.

## Questions

If you have a question about your information, or want to ask us to see, correct or delete it, email [info@nanpantanscouts.org.uk](mailto:info@nanpantanscouts.org.uk).
