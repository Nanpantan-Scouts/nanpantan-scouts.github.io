Cubs is for young people aged 8 to 10½. Develop new skills, soar to great heights: being a Cub opens up a whole other world.

{{< section-facts >}}

## What do Cubs do?

Cubs take on bigger challenges: camping, outdoor skills, cooking, cycling, and activities like climbing and abseiling. They work in small groups called sixes, learn to look after each other, and earn badges for the things they try.

## Joining Cubs

Cubs places go to young people moving up from our Beavers first. If a space does come up, we advertise it on our [Facebook page](https://www.facebook.com/NanpantanScouts) and offer it first come, first served. This doesn't happen often, so the surest way to a place is to [join our waiting list](/join/) while your child is young.

At 10½, Cubs move up to [Scouts](/scouts/).

Find out more about Cubs, including their badges, on the [Scouts website](https://www.scouts.org.uk/cubs).
