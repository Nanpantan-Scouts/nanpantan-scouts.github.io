Squirrels is the youngest section in Scouting, for children aged 4 to 6. It's where most young people start their adventure with us.

{{< section-facts >}}

## What do Squirrels do?

Squirrels meetings are full of games, stories, crafts and activities, indoors and out. Children make friends, try new things, and earn their first badges, all at a pace that suits younger children.

## Joining Squirrels

Squirrels places come from our waiting list, and **only children under 4 can be added to it**, so get your child's name down early. When your child turns 4, we offer places in waiting-list order as they become free.

{{< join-button "Join the waiting list" >}}

[How our waiting lists work](/join/)

When they turn 6, Squirrels move up to [Beavers](/beavers/).

Find out more about Squirrels, including their badges, on the [Scouts website](https://www.scouts.org.uk/squirrels).
